The word

sonder

noun

the realization that everyone has a life as vivid and complicated as your own.

Every person you sit across a table from is there because of every decision that came before that moment, a whole life, as complicated and vivid as yours, that happened to lead them to that seat.

When something is plated for you, it isn't only carrying our experience. It's also being tasted through everything you've lived, so what reaches the table is really the sum of two lives, not one. That felt worth building a kitchen around.

It's why we care about food beyond just what warms the body. Whatever shape this takes, a dish put up for the week, a supper club, food for an event, it comes from the same place: the knowledge that if we're part of someone's day, even briefly, that's worth taking seriously.
